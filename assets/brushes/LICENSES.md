# Bundled brushes

- `Compositor Basics.abr`: made by this app (`src/brush_set.rs`), same terms as the app.
- `GIMP Texture/`, `GIMP Splatters/`, `GIMP Media/`, `GIMP Sketch/`: the brush tips that ship with GIMP, as
  their original `.gbr` and `.gih` files, from https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/gimp-data (`brushes/`). GIMP's
  LICENSE file (copied here as `GIMP-LICENSE.txt`) places its brush data under CC0 (no rights reserved).

Files placed in `~/.local/share/compositor/brushes/` by hand are the user's own affair; check their terms.
